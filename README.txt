JACKPOT DEFENSE v18.8-202611011245

A casino roguelite: a slot machine for chips, Zaza's bar, the Inspector's black market,
and a pinball-flipper base defense. Last 30 rounds and break the Baron's bank.

Play
- Open en.html (English) or index.html (French). On first launch, the game picks your device's language;
  you can switch any time in OPTIONS › Game › "Langue · Language".
- Your progress and the online leaderboard are the same in both languages.
- Music played by real instruments (OPTIONS > Audio > Music style: Instruments or 8-bit).
- Achievements: PROFILE > "See achievements". Colorblind mode: OPTIONS > Graphics.
- Your first 2 runs are gentler (systems arrive a bit later, and the first machine shows only its 10 simplest chips until round 5).
- Hits feel stronger: cards recoil and flash, damage numbers, card shards, bigger explosions.
- In combat your chips fold into a compact column (or row on phones); the small arrow button opens them in full.
- When Gaston runs off, you see it happen: your gold flies to him and he dashes across the screen with his bag.
- Leaderboards go up to the top 100; tap a player with an eye to see their run (chips, stake, deck, pets).
- A health bar under your base, a "Skip tutorial" button, and redrawn characters and enemy cards.
- Much less text: every chip has one short line (more in "Details"); the market and the bar fit on one line per offer.
- "LA MAISON JOUE…" (the House plays): the reel can no longer be skipped, the rule comes in 3 steps, and stays on screen during the round.
- Crude language (OPTIONS › Game, on by default): swearing and dark humour from everyone. "No" for the clean version.
- When you lose, Gaston mocks you, together with your pet.
- New 3D intro: you ARE the chip, rolling across the table in first person; it wobbles, falls, JACKPOT DEFENSE lights up in neon and the camera pulls back over the whole table.
- Log in from the title screen (blue LOG IN button next to PROFILE). Forgot your password? Get a 6-digit code by email, type it in the game with a new password.
- Optional cloud save: create an account with your email and a password (OPTIONS › Profile). Your progress (chips, catalogue, records, skins, run in progress, leaderboard name) follows you on every device and saves by itself; settings stay per device. If two devices both moved on, the game asks which progress to keep.
- Phones: tapping a chip no longer freezes the game for seconds (its damage test now runs in the background), Mr Anatole shows up again on iPhone, no more accidental zoom (double-tap, pinch), sound comes back after a call or a locked screen, and music uses less memory.
- Fixes: the Inspector's portrait always fits on screen; the black market and end screen no longer shrink too much on large screens; leaving Zaza's bar for combat no longer jolts the screen.
- Every character now breathes, sways and changes pose on their own when nobody is talking. Anatole no longer bounces: softer motion and smooth pose changes for everyone.
- Punch-o-matic: the Inspector slips your chip into his coat and pulls it out secret. Poker hand: one of the 5 cards is a GIANT. No more scrolling on the end screen or the black market. Anatole has his own music, a sharper animated portrait and a desk that matches his library. Bigger darts game. The base health bar turns see-through under the flipper.
- Mr Anatole's notebooks now live in his own library: tall shelves, Anatole standing in front, his two notebooks on a velvet stand. Music now waits for the intro to finish. Regulars visit once every 10 rounds. Characters leave in a puff of smoke instead of folding. The Shell game stops dealing when the table is empty.
- New "Casino Noir" portraits for Zaza, the Inspector, the Baron, Madame Nox, Dina, Momo and Mr Anatole, each with a casino symbol and 10 poses. Like in Hades, they change faces with every line: they laugh when you lose, fume when you win, whisper their secrets. On PC they stand tall beside the bar and the black market; new lines written for each pose.
- Each first-person room now matches its owner: Nox's moon, velvet drapes and floating tarot cards, Dina's dartboard and neon tags, Momo's orange 8-ball sign and cue rack, the Baron's gold sconces. A siren sweeps the black market alley.
- Poker against the Baron is now first person (Buckshot Roulette style): his dark table, a flickering lamp, him facing you. He deals to you, the camera closes in on the reveal, he leans in when he wins and fades into the shadows when you do. On phones, the hand ranking is behind the "Hands" button. Reduced motion keeps the old view.
- The title screen tilts toward your mouse (or finger), each layer moves at its own speed and the background swirls around the pointer. Menu buttons grow and wobble on hover, squash and bounce on click, with a flash and a little screen bump.
- Loaded dice, new rule: every 5 s the croupier throws. Under 10: cards charge at you for 3 s. 10 or more: they back off for 3 s. Your BALL still rerolls a die. He stops when every card is down.
- Black Joker and the High Roller redrawn. The chip rack no longer overflows with Buffer or decks that add slots. Zaza's shots get pricier as rounds go up (still worth it).
- Against the Baron, the hand ranking can be hidden or shown with a button (your choice is kept). Fixed: the defeat screen was stuck on the left and cut off on PC since v16.0.
- Daily challenge: the same run for everyone each day, with its own leaderboard ("Daily" tab).

Put it online (free) and install it
1. Upload ALL the files of this folder to a static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages…).
   index.html and en.html must be at the root, with the "music" folder next to them.
2. Open the address:
   - iPhone: Safari > Share > "Add to Home Screen"
   - Android: Chrome > menu ⋮ > "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen")
   - PC: Chrome or Edge > "Install" icon in the address bar
3. After the first visit, the game also works offline.

Move your progress to another device: PROFILE > "Records and save" > "Copy my code", then "Load this code".
